About Us

Welcome to Pawsome Pups Doggy Daycare and Hotel

Pawsome Pups Doggy Daycare and Hotel is a dog daycare and overnight care facility located at 447 Jan Smuts Avenue, Blairgowrie, Randburg. It caters to dog owners who need a safe, supervised environment for their pets during the day or while they are away. We offer daycare services and sleepover facilities. Please contact us regarding availability and rates.

Operating Hours:

Monday to Friday: 07h00-17h30

Saturday to Sunday: 08h00-16h00

Please call to check if we are available that weekend.
